我们如何处理你的资料

不二见己默认在设备上计算和生图。隐私模式、云端图谱保存和匿名产品统计默认关闭；说明书记录默认开启，未登录时仅保存在本机，登录后自动同步到账号。隐私模式关闭时，生成的图片默认显示姓名、日期、时间和地点。未登录时开启历史不会上传；登录后已保存内容会自动账号同步。

账号登录与私密日记

你可以选择使用 Google 或 Apple 登录不二账号。我们通过独立的不二见己 Supabase 项目处理身份验证，不与三慢问道或其他产品共用用户数据库。身份服务会保存账号标识，以及登录提供方在你授权后提供的邮箱、显示名称和头像等基础资料；我们不接收 Google 或 Apple 的账号密码。请在 H5 与 App 使用同一种登录方式，以继续使用同一个不二账号。

私密日记会先缓存在当前设备，并同步到你的不二账号，以便在已登录的 H5 与 App 中继续编辑。传输使用 HTTPS；日记不是端到端加密内容。日记正文、标题、心情与日期不会进入产品统计。默认不发送给 AI；在「身边的人」中首次明确授权后，可按问题检索相关摘录用于关系对话。授权按账号保存，你可以随时关闭或在发送前取消本次参考。

私密关系档案

你可以为自己、家人或合作伙伴建立私密人物档案。录入他人的出生资料前，应取得本人允许；未成年子女资料由监护人管理。昵称、关系标签、出生资料、人类图和你的观察保存在你的账号内，不向该人物或其他用户公开。关系档案与关系对话同步到同一账号的 H5 与 App，使用 HTTPS 传输，不是端到端加密。

发起关系对话时，服务端核对当前账号与所选人物所有权，只把对方图谱摘要、你授权的本人图谱、成长访谈、经历、行动复盘、日记与历史对话的相关摘录发送给 第三方 AI 服务。对方姓名、完整出生信息、人物备注和其他人物的独立档案不自动发送；用户自己的文字可能包含主动写入的个人信息。普通对话、成长档案与说明书在登录后自动账号同步；用于 AI 的参考快照及来源范围独立管理，只有主动生成、更新或发送问题时才按授权使用。每次检索最近 1000 篇云端日记和与该人物最近 30 段关系对话中的相关片段，不代表完整读取生活史。你可关闭来源，删除人物及关联对话；删除账号同时删除其人物、参考快照、关系对话及云端日记。

你可以导出日记、退出或切换账号，并在账号页发起删除账号。删除会移除不二账号及其云端日记，并清除当前设备上该账号的日记缓存；账号中的普通对话、成长档案、说明书、人物档案、关系对话和相处指南也会删除，并移除此设备上该账号的相关缓存；其他离线设备需联网后接收删除状态。通过 Apple 登录时，H5 会在当前浏览器会话中临时保存 Apple 返回的访问令牌，原生 App 会在删除时重新请求一次 Apple 授权；这些凭据仅用于在删除账号时撤销 Apple 授权，不进入日记或统计。删除账号不会自动取消 App Store 订阅。

公开网站使用 Google Analytics 汇总基础访问量、页面浏览量和会话数，独立于“帮助我们改进不二见己”产品操作统计开关。基础访问统计使用最长 30 天的第一方标识 Cookie，并将技术请求信息发送给 Google；这些标识不等于完全匿名。页面地址、标题和来源经固定化处理，不发送姓名、出生资料、查询参数或完整图谱；广告个性化和 Google Signals 关闭。此基础访问统计不在原生 App 中运行。

在普通 HTTP 页面且不是 Capacitor 原生应用时，不二见己会强制进入临时本地模式：云端保存、匿名统计、云端删除和 Supabase 匿名身份均停用。远程资料功能只在安全上下文或原生运行环境中可用。纯 JavaScript SHA-256 fallback 只用于保持本地 Hash 计算兼容，不提供传输加密，也不能替代 HTTPS。

与账号同步分开的匿名图谱云存储，仅在你单独、明确开启相关开关后，新生成的姓名、出生资料、时区、图谱快照及版本信息才会上传。匿名统计也需要单独、明确开启“帮助我们改进不二见己”。开启后，网站会向我们的产品统计服务与 Google Analytics 发送经白名单限制的操作事件；这些事件不包含姓名、出生资料、地点、完整图谱、自由文本或错误堆栈。Google 会依据其隐私条款处理相关请求。

为了把你输入的出生地点转换为计算所需的坐标和时区，应用会把你主动输入的地点搜索文字发送给 Photon；服务不可用时可能使用 ArcGIS Geocoding 作为备用。我们不会随地点文字一同发送姓名、出生日期或生成结果。该文字是你手动输入的出生地点，并非你或设备的当前定位。第三方服务可能按照各自政策处理或保留网络请求；提交 App Store 前，我们仍需确认其保留政策并据此完成最终隐私分类。

AI 对话

你主动发送问题时，问题和本轮所需的近期对话会经过我们的服务端转发给 第三方 AI 服务 生成回应。只有勾选“结合成长档案”时才会附带类型、策略、内在权威与人生角色；不会自动附带姓名或出生资料。请按自己的意愿选择输入内容。对话不计入说明书历史开关，未登录时保存在本机，登录后自动同步到账号，不再按最近 20 组截断；可在“对话记录”中清空。我们的对话服务不持久保存问题和回复；第三方 AI 服务 的处理与保留适用其自身隐私政策。关闭本机历史不会撤回已发出的请求。

全量账号同步

普通对话、成长档案和已保存说明书与日记、人物及关系资料一起按账号隔离保存。首次登录会直接合并此设备尚未归属账号的旧记录，不再弹出确认；已归属另一账号的缓存不会合并。离线更改先存本机，重新联网后重试；同时编辑产生的不同版本会保留，供导出或恢复。删除记录会同步到同一账号的其他设备。同步不等于 AI 授权，不会因此把全部资料发送给 AI，也不复制登录凭据、会员额度或匿名统计标识。同步内容不是端到端加密。

成长档案与 AI 参考资料

访谈回答、AI 行动指南、经历和行动复盘先保存在当前设备，登录后自动同步到账号，独立于人类图历史设置。经历可逐条编辑、删除，成长档案页支持导出与清空。生成行动指南时，12 个访谈回答会发送至我们的服务器与 第三方 AI 服务。日常对话只有勾选“结合成长档案”时才附带你允许参考的回答和最多 4 条相关经历（每条前 2000 字符）。你写入这些内容的个人信息也会随内容发送。关闭授权会停止后续附带，不会撤回已发送的请求。AI 不会自动读取手机其他资料，也不保证记住全部经历。清空说明书历史不清空成长档案；请在成长档案中单独管理。

设备权限

  • 相册：仅在你点击“保存图片”时请求，用于把生成图片写入系统相册。
  • 系统分享：仅在你主动分享时调用，由 Apple 的分享面板决定接收方。

保留与删除

说明书记录默认开启，生成结果先存本机，登录后同步到账号。关闭该开关时，应用提供取消、关闭但保留已有记录、关闭并删除全部记录三种选择。选择保留时仍可打开和逐条删除旧记录，但新结果不会继续加入历史。卸载应用也会移除本地历史。“删除云端图谱与个人资料”会在同一事务中删除当前匿名身份保存的姓名、出生资料、图谱、授权记录与云端资料，不会删除本设备历史。已经记录的匿名使用事件会移除用户标识，并最多保留 180 天用于汇总统计；不含出生资料的删除收据最多保留 365 天。匿名图谱云存储需单独删除；账号中的说明书、成长档案和普通对话删除会同步到所有设备。

儿童与健康声明

本应用面向 13 岁及以上用户，用于自我探索与娱乐，不提供医疗、心理、法律或财务诊断与建议。

可选使用统计 / Optional usage analytics

“分享使用统计，帮助豆豆龙改进”是网站与 App 独立于云端保存的新开关，默认关闭，不沿用旧授权。开启后，Google Analytics / Firebase 会处理假名化安装或浏览器标识、设备技术信息、页面类别、前台活跃时长及功能操作结果，例如对话请求、完成、失败与耗时，说明书生成、访谈保存次数、经历与行动保存、分享及会员操作。它们不包含对话内容、出生资料、访谈回答、经历正文、交易标识或错误堆栈，也不代表完全匿名。我们不设置用户身份，不启用广告跟踪、Google Signals、IDFA 或 IDFV 采集。统计不用于广告。

可随时在隐私设置关闭；关闭停止后续产品事件并丢弃未发出的本地队列，App 同时重置统计标识。关闭不会撤回已提交给 Google 的汇总数据。网站基础访问量统计仍按本政策前文说明独立运行。原生 App 只对经验证的 App Store 正式安装启用统计，开发、模拟器及 TestFlight 不计入正式使用数据。iOS 15 不启用此统计。统计授权不影响任何免费功能或订阅权益。

The separate Share usage statistics control is off by default and does not reuse previous consent. When enabled, Google Analytics / Firebase processes pseudonymous installation/browser identifiers, technical device information, screen categories, foreground active duration and feature outcomes. These include chat requests, completions, errors and latency, manual generation, reflection saves, stories, actions, sharing and membership actions. No chat text, birth details, answers, stories, transaction identifiers or raw error stacks are sent. These identifiers are not fully anonymous. We do not set user identity or enable advertising, Google Signals, IDFA or IDFV collection.

Turn this control off at any time to stop subsequent product events and discard unsent local events. The app also resets its analytics identifier. Previously submitted aggregate data is not recalled. Basic H5 audience measurement remains separate as described above. Native analytics only runs for verified production App Store installations, not development, simulators or TestFlight, and is disabled on iOS 15. Consent does not affect features or membership.

ENGLISH

How we handle your information

Buer Within calculates and renders locally by default. Privacy mode, anonymous cloud chart saving, and optional product analytics are off by default. History is on by default and saved on this device first. With privacy mode off, generated images show the entered name, date, time, and location. When signed out, local history is not uploaded; signed-in saved records automatically sync to the account.

Account sign-in and private journal

You may sign in to a Buer account with Google or Apple. Authentication is handled by a dedicated Buer Within Supabase project; user records are not shared with Wendao or our other products. The identity service stores an account identifier and basic profile data that the provider releases with your consent, such as an email address, display name, or avatar. We never receive your Google or Apple password. Use the same sign-in method on H5 and the app to continue with the same Buer account.

Private journals are cached on the device and synced to your Buer account over HTTPS, not end-to-end encryption. Their titles, text, mood and dates are excluded from analytics. Journals are not sent to AI by default. After explicit first-time authorization in People, relevant excerpts may be retrieved for relationship conversations. You can revoke this account permission or disable a source before sending.

Private relationship profiles

You can save profiles for yourself, family and partners in your private account. Obtain permission before entering another adult's birth details; a guardian manages a child's information. Nicknames, relationship labels, birth details, chart results and your observations are not shared with the person or other users. Profiles and relationship conversations sync between web and App using HTTPS; they are not end-to-end encrypted.

The server verifies ownership of the selected person and personal context. Their chart summary and relevant excerpts from your authorized chart, growth reflections, experiences, actions, journals and history are sent to a third-party AI service. Other people's separate profiles, the selected person's name, birth details and private notes are not automatically included. Your own writing may contain personal information you entered. Growth records and general chats sync automatically after sign-in. AI reference snapshots and source scopes remain separately controlled and are used only when you explicitly generate, update or send a request. Retrieval is bounded to relevant excerpts from the latest 1,000 cloud journals and 30 conversations with this person, not an entire life history. Account deletion cascades to profiles, reference snapshots, relationship conversations and cloud journals.

You can export your journal, sign out, switch accounts, or initiate account deletion from the account screen. Deletion removes the Buer account and cloud journal and clears that account's journal cache on the current device. Account chats, growth records, manuals, people, relationship conversations and guides are also deleted, together with their cache on this device. Other offline devices must reconnect to receive deletion. For Sign in with Apple, H5 temporarily keeps the Apple access token for the current browser session, while the native app requests fresh Apple authorization during deletion. These credentials are used only to revoke Apple authorization when deleting the account and are excluded from journals and analytics. Deleting the account does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription.

The public website uses Google Analytics to aggregate basic visits, page views, and sessions independently of the Help Us Improve Buer Within interaction setting. Basic measurement uses first-party identifier cookies lasting up to 30 days and sends technical request information to Google; these identifiers are not fully anonymous. Page address, title, and referrer are fixed to exclude names, birth details, query parameters, and complete charts. Ad personalization and Google Signals are disabled. This basic website measurement does not run in the native app.

On an ordinary HTTP page outside the Capacitor native app, Buer Within enforces temporary local-only mode: cloud saving, analytics, cloud deletion, and Supabase anonymous identity are disabled. Remote data features require a secure context or the native runtime. The pure JavaScript SHA-256 fallback only preserves compatible local hashing; it does not encrypt transport and is not a substitute for HTTPS.

For the separate anonymous chart store, only after you explicitly enable Cloud Save may new names, birth details, time zones, snapshots, and versions be uploaded. Anonymous analytics also require separately enabling Help Us Improve Buer Within. When enabled, the website sends allowlisted interaction events to our product analytics service and Google Analytics; these events exclude names, birth details, locations, complete charts, free text, and raw stacks. Google processes those requests under its own privacy terms.

To convert a place you enter into coordinates and a time zone, the app sends only your place-search text to Photon and may use ArcGIS Geocoding as a fallback. We do not send your name, birth date, or generated result with that query. The text is a manually entered birth place, not the current location of you or your device. These providers may process or retain requests under their own policies; their retention behavior must still be confirmed before the final App Store privacy classification.

AI conversations

When you send a question, our server forwards it and the recent conversation context to a third-party AI service to generate a response. Only when you select Use my growth profile do we include type, strategy, inner authority and profile. Names and birth details are not attached automatically. Conversation history is separate from the Life Manual history setting and is saved locally when signed out and synced after sign-in without a 20-conversation truncation. You can clear it under Conversations. Our chat service does not persist questions or answers; Third-party AI processing and retention follow its own privacy policy. Deleting local history does not recall requests already sent.

Full account synchronization

General chats, growth records and saved manuals are isolated by account alongside journals, people and relationship data. First sign-in automatically merges this device’s unowned records without another prompt; another account’s cache is never imported. Offline edits are queued; concurrent versions are preserved for export or recovery. Deletions sync across devices. Sync does not authorize AI access or copy credentials, quota or analytics identities. Synced content is not end-to-end encrypted.

Growth profile and AI context

Reflection answers, AI guides, stories, actions and reviews are saved locally and synced after sign-in, independently of the reading-history setting. Edit or delete stories individually, or export and clear the profile from Growth. Generating a guide sends the 12 reflection answers to our server and a third-party AI service. Chat only attaches permitted answers and up to 4 relevant stories (first 2,000 characters each) when you enable profile context. Personal information you enter in shared content is transmitted too. Revoking permission stops future attachments but cannot recall past requests. AI does not read other device data automatically or remember every life experience. Clearing reading history does not clear the growth profile.

Device permissions

  • Photos: requested only when you choose Save Image, solely to add the generated image to your library.
  • System sharing: opened only when you choose Share; recipients are controlled by Apple's share sheet.

Retention and deletion

History is on by default; generated Life Manuals are saved locally and synced after sign-in. Turning it off offers three choices: cancel, stop future saves while keeping existing records, or stop and delete all records. Kept records remain available to open or delete individually, while new results are not added. Uninstalling also removes local history. Delete Cloud Charts and Personal Data atomically removes the current anonymous identity's saved name, birth details, charts, consent records, and cloud profile, but not local history. Previously recorded usage events are deidentified and retained for no more than 180 days for aggregate statistics. Birth-data-free deletion receipts are retained for no more than 365 days. Anonymous cloud charts are separate; deleting account manuals, growth records or chats propagates across devices.
