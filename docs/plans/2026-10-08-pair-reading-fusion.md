# Relationship guide: personal reading first

User requested directly merging the strengths of the old and new guides and publishing.

Comparison: older guides placed personal prose directly in the reading flow. V2 introduced repeated generic cards before the prose and collapsed old readings; reusing the friction key also mislabeled older support/friction text as decisions.

Selected: keep concise sticky navigation, account persistence and expandable technical evidence. Restore direct reading of all saved prose; show no generic cards when a personal section exists. Overview starts with a compact three-row comparison (type, authority, profile), then prose; full facts remain below in a disclosure. Un-generated sections retain a basic exercise and at most one connection prompt. Legacy saved content keeps its original section order/meanings; new generation remains six-part but requests coherent, specific analysis instead of repeated exercises. Do not regenerate, migrate or overwrite private saved content as part of deployment.

Reject a full rollback (loses navigation/accessibility improvements) and simply adding more cards (keeps the same clutter). No database/auth changes or AI calls required. Verify both legacy and v2 fixtures, all-category visibility, mobile overflow, unit suite and public H5 artifact.
