# 不二见己：专属 AI 成长教练

## Product decision
Keep 不二见己 / Buer Within as the brand. Position it as “你的专属 AI 成长教练 / Your personal AI growth coach”. A strategist is too narrow; a life mentor suggests authority over the user's decisions. The coach helps users understand patterns, choose an action and review lived results.

The user's September 25 brief authorizes implementing three stages: Human Design first, an optional resumable HUMAN 3.0 reflection second, and an expandable personal history third. Preserve existing readings and subscription entitlements. Do not change Pluto or submit a new review as part of this implementation.

## Journey and placement
- Home: coach conversation, an entry to the three-stage growth profile, and next action.
- Growth profile (replaces the navigation label Life Manual): Human Design entry / four-domain reflection / personal history, followed by actions and reviews.
- Existing Human Design creation/result pages remain, with a next-step entry to the growth profile.
- Me: growth profile entry, existing reading history, conversations, settings.

The assessment is a 12-question baseline (three questions per domain), one question at a time, freely editable, resumable and skippable. A deliberate Generate button sends answers to AI and consumes one completed AI-reply allowance. The coach may ask a follow-up in normal conversation. Do not claim this is the full 12–32-question adaptive source interview.

AI synthesis: summarize evidence in mind, body, relationships/meaning and vocation, distinguish reported facts from tentative interpretations, identify one bottleneck, propose a next-24-hours experiment, a 30-day practice and a 90-day checkpoint. Users write their chosen action, due date and success criterion, then mark completion and record reflection. No automated consciousness score or deterministic personality label.

## Personal context and privacy
Local, versioned storage for answers, generated synthesis, up to 100 user-authored story entries and 100 actions. Text/Markdown imports and paste are supported with visible limits. Notes have title, body and permission to be used by AI. Users can edit/delete/export records and clear the growth profile with confirmation. No background uploads; an explicit conversation checkbox controls whether selected context is sent. Limit transmission to relevant selected entries and disclose that it is a subset, not perfect memory of an entire life. Never claim the AI understands users better than everyone else.

Context is sent as a distinct bounded user-data message; system instructions remain separate. Validate every field on the server. Birth name/date/time/place are excluded from automatically attached Human Design context. Source stories may contain user-entered personal details; refresh AI consent and privacy copy accordingly. Assessment synthesis is stored separately from original answers and is invalidated by answer changes.
