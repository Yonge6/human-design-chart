常见问题

成长档案如何开始？

先填写人类图信息，再选择是否进行 HUMAN 3.0 四领域访谈。访谈可跳过或随时续答；个人经历可随时补充。完成 12 个基础问题后，可以生成 AI 行动指南，再记录你选择的行动、回顾日期和进展。

AI 如何参考我的经历？

保存经历时选择是否允许 AI 参考，并在对话中勾选“结合成长档案”。每次只发送部分相关内容，不会后台上传全部档案。可导入 TXT / Markdown 或直接粘贴；每条最多 4000 字符。PDF、照片和跨设备同步暂不支持。

免费额度如何计算？

每天可免费获得 3 条完整 AI 回复。生成一份完整行动指南也计为 1 条；失败不扣额度。填写访谈和管理本机档案不需要订阅。

地点搜不到怎么办？

可以直接输入“城市 + 省/州 + 国家”，无需一定点击候选项。例如：湘潭市雨湖区，中国。应用会优先使用搜索结果，无法联网时也会根据完整文字尝试继续。

为什么出生时间很重要？

几分钟通常不会改变大部分结果，但临近闸门或线位边界时可能产生差异。请尽量使用出生证明上的当地时间。

如何保存或分享？

生成后点击“保存图片”写入系统相册，或点击“分享”打开 iOS 分享面板。首次保存时请允许相册添加权限。

如何删除我的资料？

在“我的”中打开隐私设置，可清空说明书历史。成长档案与对话需在各自页面单独删除。卸载应用也会删除保存在应用沙盒中的资料。

结果是否是专业诊断？

不是。内容用于自我探索与娱乐，不应替代医疗、心理、法律、财务或职业建议。

Frequently asked questions

How do I build my growth profile?

Begin with your Human Design reading, then optionally reflect across four HUMAN 3.0 domains. Pause and resume the 12 starting questions whenever you need. Add personal stories at any time. Generate an AI guide, choose an action and record a review date and progress.

How does AI use my stories?

Allow individual entries as context, then enable profile context in chat. Only a relevant subset is attached to each request. Paste text or import TXT / Markdown, up to 4,000 characters per entry. PDF, photo imports and cross-device sync are not supported yet.

What counts toward the free allowance?

You receive 3 completed AI replies daily. A completed action guide counts as one reply; failed requests do not count. Local reflections and profile management need no subscription.

What if my place is not found?

Enter a complete place such as city, state or province, and country. Selecting a suggestion is optional. The app uses search results when available and can still try to continue from complete text when the network is unavailable.

Why does birth time matter?

A few minutes usually do not change most results, but a time near a gate or line boundary can. Use the local time shown on a birth record whenever possible.

How do I save or share?

After generating, choose Save Image to add the image to Photos or Share to open the iOS share sheet. Allow add-only Photos access when prompted.

How do I delete my data?

Open Privacy Settings to clear reading history. Delete the growth profile and conversations separately from their own pages. Uninstalling the app also removes data stored in its app container.

Is this professional advice?

No. The content is for personal reflection and entertainment and is not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal, financial, or career advice.
