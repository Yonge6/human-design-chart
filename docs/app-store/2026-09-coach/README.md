# Buer Within — growth coach revision

Status: product revision deployed to H5 and the independent AI service; iOS simulator build passed. Store metadata remains a candidate, not uploaded or submitted to App Store Connect.
Previous 1.0(1) review and poster package remain in `../2026-09-release/` as historical evidence.

Proposed names:
- 不二见己：你的AI成长教练
- Buer Within: AI Growth Coach

Positioning: Understand your own context, choose a practical next step, and learn from action. Human Design is the initial interpretive lens within a broader personal growth profile. HUMAN 3.0 is a credited reflection framework, not a scientific/clinical measurement or an endorsed official assessment.

Implemented scope:
- Growth tab with three stages and an explicit return to the original Human Design flow.
- Twelve original bilingual starting questions across four domains, one at a time, with local resume/revision and an optional skip to stories. This is a baseline reflection, not the full source prompt's adaptive 12–32-question interview.
- Explicit AI synthesis request with four-domain evidence, a tentative cross-domain bottleneck and 24-hour/30-day/90-day action suggestions.
- Local editable stories, TXT/Markdown import, per-entry AI permission, bounded relevant retrieval only when the user also enables profile context in chat.
- User-authored actions, progress criteria, review dates, completion and reflection. “Reflect with my coach” creates an editable message; the user must send it.
- Local export / clear; iOS export uses the system share sheet. Birth information is not automatically transmitted. AI consent v2 describes expanded context.
- Bilingual app/about/support/privacy copy and revised store metadata drafts.

Unchanged commercial terms: free 3 completed AI replies/day (UTC reset), one completed guide counts as one reply, failed replies refunded. Paid members have no daily cap with anti-abuse limits. US monthly $9.99 / annual $89.99; China monthly ¥19.90 / annual ¥159.00. No trial added.
